# beta 2026-10-15

**Everything in v2.4.6 with the campaign map, Armory Studio and the unit card
generator switched on.** Armory Studio is new on this line: a sixth tab of the
Models Editor where one model is built out of the parts of others, from any
installed mod or a `.obj`, `.glb`, `.dae` or `.mesh` file.

Armory Studio pulls parts in and lists them by group type; moves, turns and
scales them with Blender's mouse and keys and a view gizmo; recolours them with
channels and detected layers and stamps emblems on them; transfers weights,
splits, merges and renames in place, with undo and redo; previews the
animations or stands the model in its T-pose; and exports it as a `.mesh` with
new sheets, the islands cropped to their exact UV shapes and packed into each
other's corners, with normal maps, straight into a mod as a new entry or unit.
Models are kept in a library, and work is kept as named sessions with backups
every three minutes, and a warning after a crash.

The Unit cards tab has a card maker beside Clean up: the Armory Studio model
posed by preset, by any animation, or by the Blender toolkit's IK rig and its
library of 212 poses (whole or by body part, the controls dragged in the HD
view), lit, rendered at up to 40x with an outline, and scaled down to a game
card that goes straight to a unit.

Nothing on the map itself changed since beta 2026-10-11.
